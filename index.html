About Me

I'm an Electronics Engineer with a MASc in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Waterloo, specializing in systems and controls with a focus on robotics. My experience spans embedded software, electronics design, PCB development, hardware testing, and system troubleshooting. I enjoy project-based, hands-on engineering where I can understand a system from the hardware up, build and test solutions, and work through problems when things don't behave as expected. I'm particularly interested in embedded systems, hardware development, robotics, and mechatronics, and I'm always looking for opportunities where I can contribute my existing skills while continuing to learn and grow.
